RAGE Emblem Codec includes an original codec, search engine, UI, and tests.

The Rockstar-compatible strip encoding idea — raster artwork reconstructed as
SVG linear gradients plus emblem layer JSON — was demonstrated by
Flashback-GTA's Emblem Helper 1.1. That work is credited here as the
compatibility foundation. RAGE is not a reskin of that application.
